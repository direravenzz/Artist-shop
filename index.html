Artisan Spotlight

Maryam Karimi
Master of Isfahan Blue

For over 30 years, Maryam has painted the intricate floral motifs of Isfahan's famous blue-and-white ceramics in her family workshop on the banks of the Zayandeh River. Each piece takes weeks to complete — sketched freehand, fired three times, and glazed with mineral pigments unchanged since the Safavid era.

Who We Are

A Bridge Between Ancient
Hands & Modern Homes

Ārtisān Persia was founded to give master craftspeople in Iran a global stage. We connect buyers worldwide with artisans in Isfahan, Tabriz, Shiraz, and beyond — ensuring every purchase is authentic, ethically sourced, and directly benefits the maker's family.

We believe cultural heritage is best preserved through commerce. By making Persian craftsmanship accessible globally, we help sustain traditions that are thousands of years old while empowering artisan families with fair, direct income — no middlemen, no compromises.
